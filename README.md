# Gen-Z Career Aspirations – Data Analytics Project

Project Description

This project analyzes the career aspirations, workplace preferences, salary expectations, leadership expectations, learning goals, and values of 10,740 Gen-Z respondents.

The goal is to transform survey data into clear, actionable business insights that can help HR teams, recruiters, managers, and business leaders better understand what Gen-Z expects from employers.

## ## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, five key recommendations were identified:

### 1. Design for Flexibility
Offer credible remote and hybrid working options where the role permits.

### 2. Build Manager Clarity
Train managers to clearly communicate expectations, goals, and direction.

### 3. Make Mission Tangible
Ensure company values and mission are reflected in real policies and employee experiences.

### 4. Show Growth Pathways
Clearly communicate learning opportunities, career progression, and development paths.

### 5. Use Data Continuously
Continue using dashboards and future survey data to monitor changing Gen-Z expectations.

## ❓ Key Business Questions

The analysis addresses questions such as:

1. What industries are Gen-Z most interested in pursuing careers in?
2. What factors influence Gen-Z's career choices?
3. What work environment does Gen-Z prefer?
4. How do salary expectations impact career aspirations?
5. What role do personal values and social impact play in career choices?
6. What leadership qualities does Gen-Z value?
7. How important is company mission and mission-action alignment?
8. What can employers do to attract and retain Gen-Z talent?
## ⭐ Most Surprising Insight

The most surprising finding was the importance of **mission authenticity**.

**8,175 respondents** said they would not work for a company when its actions did not align with its mission.

This shows that simply communicating company values may not be enough. Employers need to demonstrate those values through actual policies and employee experiences.
## 📌 Overall Conclusion

The analysis shows that Gen-Z is not looking for a single benefit.

Their expectations combine:

**Flexibility + Clarity + Growth + Purpose + Reward**

Employers that understand and respond to these expectations can strengthen their approach to **Gen-Z recruitment, employer branding, manager development, and retention**.
